- **Sample-simulate-select (S3) draws N candidates per prompt from a frozen text-to-motion...** · score 0.087
  - _Contributions:_ The paper introduces S3, a training-free pipeline that composes a frozen text-to-motion generator, a deterministic IK retargeter, and a physics simulator running the exact deployment tracking policy as a best-of-N selector.
