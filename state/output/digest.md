- **DART starts from a rejected greedy trace, has the model segment it into reasoning steps...** · score 0.081
  - _Contributions:_ The paper introduces DART, a trace-mining method that revisits rejected reasoning traces, branches at uncertain steps with deliberately diverse alternatives, and completes them to find verifier-accepted traces. At a matched number of generated traces it recovers 37% more greedy-unsolved DeepMath-103K problems than MC sampling at about 30% lower cost per accepted trace. SFT of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on DART-mined traces gives the best point estimate in five of six MATH-500/AIME evaluations.
  - _Applicable to our work:_ Directly maps to hexgen's rejection-sampled SFT/repair-distillation loop: when all best-of-N programs fail the frozen verifier, take a failed rollout, rank its DSL commands by position-weighted model uncertainty (G-NLL), resample a deliberately different command at the top-ranked step, and greedily complete — a cheap way to recover verified programs for hard (especially real-CAD, out-of-distribution) parts that pure best-of-N never solves. The analogue of 'reference-answer guidance' is the template-derived or inverse-factory ground-truth program, which can condition the alternative command during data mining. Segmentation is trivial in a DSL (command boundaries), removing DART's LLM segmentation step.
  - _Additional context:_ The 'meta-thought' alternative proposal relies on an instruction-following LLM; for a 27M decoder, replace with constrained resampling (e.g., exclude the original token / top-k alternatives at that command). Complements BRIAR (also tonight) which does branching at bifurcation points during RL.

  - _Contributions:_ PhysMend is a training-free framework for verification-guided program refinement in physics-aware video generation. It verifies and locally repairs an editable physical program on its simulated rollout and depth control rather than on generated videos, using a hybrid verifier in which VLM judgments cannot override failed physical measurements. Failed checks localize repairable fields and residual-scaled bounded updates fix them, with the generator called once per input; it reports state-of-the-art results on Physics-IQ and PhyGenBench.
  - _Applicable to our work:_ Mechanism worth borrowing for hexgen repair: give each verifier check (inverted elements, scaled-Jacobian, surface-match, feature-edge capture) an explicit 'field ownership' map to the DSL commands/coordinate tokens that produced the offending elements, then do localized edits of only those fields and re-run the full frozen verifier with rollback, instead of resampling whole programs best-of-N. Their ablation (localized repair > global rebuild > no repair) is a direct analog of edit-repair vs re-generation for broken programs, and the trajectories could feed a DAgger-style repair-distillation dataset. Their parameter updates are continuous and heuristic (per-check direction rules); our coordinate vocabulary is discrete, so updates would be neighbor-token moves. - **Briar builds on-policy, discourse-segmented reasoning trees, expanding nodes along a UC...** · score 0.024
  - _Contributions:_ The paper shows that the prefix pass probability under an on-policy verifier is a bounded Doob martingale whose step-credit variance is conserved and vanishes at derailed and absorbed boundaries. Building on this, it proposes Briar, a tree-structured RLVR framework that steers branching toward unresolved decision points, computes exact linear-time posterior node values via RTS smoothing, and applies conciseness advantages only post-absorption. Across four 1.7B-8B backbones and five reasoning benchmarks it reports accuracy gains over DAPO with shorter responses and fewer GPU-hours.
  - _Applicable to our work:_ Hexgen programs are verified by a frozen, deterministic checker, so the prefix pass probability V(prefix) of a partial DSL program is exactly the quantity Briar models. Transferable pieces: (1) branch best-of-N/beam search at program-prefix 'bifurcation points' (estimated pass rate in ~[0.15,0.85]) instead of sampling N full programs from scratch, which concentrates compute on the command where validity is decided; (2) RTS tree smoothing as a cheap, principled estimator of per-prefix value from a handful of verified rollouts, usable as training targets for a value/critic head that guides decoding; (3) natural segmentation at DSL command boundaries replaces discourse segmentation. The conciseness/absorption part maps loosely to element-budget or program-length regularization.
  - _Additional context:_ Developed for multi-thousand-token LLM math reasoning with RL (DAPO baseline), whereas hexgen is a 27M supervised model with short programs; the RL policy-update machinery is the least transferable part, but the frontier-selection and value-smoothing components can be used purely at data-generation/search time. Results are against RLVR baselines on public math benchmarks; no code link found in the text. Related to VinePPO / TreeRL lines of prefix-value estimation.
