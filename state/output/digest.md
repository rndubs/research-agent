  - _Applicable to our work:_ The core transferable mechanism is the localized re-masking/infilling repair strategy: instead of re-rolling out an entire generated program when it fails, DiffPDE uses execution feedback to localize the offending span (their deterministic traceback-anchored procedure: find the failing line, expand to the nearest natural boundary such as a blank/comment line or shallower indentation, then only regenerate that span) and repairs just that region under full surrounding context. This maps directly onto our 'repair/edit distillation for fixing broken program rollouts' care area: our frozen verifier already returns structured failure diagnostics (compile errors, inverted elements, quality-bound violations, etc.) that could be used analogously to localize which block/sweep/projection command(s) in a generated hexgen program are responsible, and train/decode a targeted fix for just that span rather than regenerating the whole program from scratch. Third, their task-allocation/dynamic-buffer curriculum (annealing from mostly synthetic 'constructed' infilling tasks toward mostly real error-driven infilling tasks) is a concrete, DAgger-adjacent recipe for how to schedule a repair/edit-distillation curriculum against our own inverse-factory-generated vs. verifier-caught-real-failure data.
